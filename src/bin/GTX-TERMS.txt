1. What GTX Browser is
   GTX Browser is a free desktop program for Windows that displays web
   pages using the WebView2 engine that Windows already ships. It is not
   sold, it is not a service, and it is not affiliated with Microsoft or
   DuckDuckGo.

2. Your data stays on your computer
   GTX Browser does not send your browsing history, bookmarks, downloads
   or settings to us - there is no GTX server. History, settings, logs
   and the download list are stored in plain files in your Windows user
   profile (the "GTX Browser" folder under AppData\Local), and you can
   delete them at any time.

3. Web content comes from other people
   Pages, search results, downloads and certificates are provided by the
   websites you visit. GTX Browser does not create, check or endorse that
   content. Visiting a site shares the normal technical information every
   browser must share (such as your IP address and browser name) with
   that site.

4. Downloads are your responsibility
   Files you download are saved where you choose. GTX Browser records
   the file name, source address, time and result in a local list; it
   does not scan files for viruses. Keep your own protection up to date
   and only download files you trust.

5. Search default
   The default search and the address suggestions use DuckDuckGo. You can
   change this in Settings. Other sites have their own terms and privacy
